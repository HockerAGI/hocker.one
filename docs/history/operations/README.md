# HOCKER ONE — Historical operations archive

This directory preserves superseded operational checkpoints, reconciliation notes and addenda for audit/history.

Files here are **not current operating authority** unless a current document explicitly revalidates and cites them.

Current sources live in `docs/operations/` and are indexed by `docs/operations/INDEX.md`.

Archive rules:

- preserve original content/blobs whenever possible;
- do not edit history to make past assertions look current;
- do not use archived green states to claim current readiness;
- migration/security/release evidence may remain historically valuable even after newer controls supersede it;
- when a historical assertion conflicts with current executable evidence, current executable evidence wins and the conflict should remain visible in history.
